# Changelog

## v1.6.1

### New

*   **The colour scale has been rebuilt around the season's actual upgrade ranks.** Gold had stopped meaning anything: measured against 184 real players in week 6, it held **58.7 %** of everyone — in a five-player group, three were gold or better. The cause was a wrong assumption rather than a generous number. Gold sat at the item level a +10 key pays out and called itself "beyond mythic+", but a fully upgraded Hero set is three levels higher than that and comes out of mythic+ alone. Every boundary now sits on a real upgrade rank, read from equipped gear rather than guessed, and gold means what it always claimed to: the first rank above a complete Hero set. No band holds more than a quarter of the field, and a new heirloom-cyan band sits above gold for mythic raid gear. The bands still hang off Blizzard's reward curve, so they keep moving with the season on their own.

*   **The bottom of the scale is usable again.** Grey sat twenty levels below the season floor and caught nobody — the lowest item level seen in a whole season's cache was still well above it. It now sits five below the floor, so grey and green each carry a real share of undergeared players instead of one being permanently empty.
